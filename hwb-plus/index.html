HWB and HWB-Plus Audio Demonstrations

VCTK evaluation samples

Listening comparison

One utterance, four signal conditions

Each row keeps the utterance fixed and compares the low-resolution input, the HWB baseline, the HWB-Plus prediction, and the high-resolution reference. Every playback control is paired with its own log-magnitude spectrogram.

Sampling note HWB is exported from its original 4 kHz to 16 kHz checkpoint using the validation-time preprocessing and STFT implementation. Within each row, all four conditions share one peak gain so that the highest amplitude is -3 dBFS. The LR, HWB-Plus, and HR channels are the supplied 22.05 kHz evaluation material.
